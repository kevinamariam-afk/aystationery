Our story

Stationery, the way Bur Dubai shops.

AY Stationery is the retail face of Abbas Yousuf Trading LLC, a stationery and office-supplies trader in the heart of Bur Dubai. We look after everyone from a parent with a back-to-school list to an office manager restocking a whole floor.

Who we are

Small enough to know you. Stocked enough to surprise you.

Walk into our store behind Khalid Bin Waleed Street and you'll find shelves running from everyday ballpoints and copier paper to watercolour sets, stretched canvases and desk equipment. We carry trusted names like Staedtler and Funbo alongside the practical basics every school and office goes through week after week.

We work on two levels at once. On the retail side, families, students and artists drop in for a few items and some friendly advice. On the wholesale side, schools, companies and resellers send us full lists, and we put together a quote and have the order ready for collection.

Beyond the shelves we also offer printing services, so one stop can cover the notebooks, the files and the printed handouts that go in them.

What we believe

Three promises behind every order.

People, not checkout pages

Every order passes through a real person who can spot a missing item, suggest a better-value alternative or tell you honestly when something is out of stock.

A range for every list

From kindergarten crayons to boardroom whiteboard markers, we'd rather say "we have it" than send you to three different shops.

Help that scales

A single notebook and a school-wide order both deserve attention. We size our help to your order, never the other way round.

How online ordering works

Order online, with a real person behind every cart.

You can now order our best sellers straight from the website: add them to your cart, check out in under a minute, and pay when you collect. But stationery orders, especially for schools and offices, are rarely just "add to cart". Quantities change, brands get swapped, and a list often needs a second pair of eyes. So every online order still lands with our team, who confirm stock, double-check quantities and get it ready for you.

About this project

Rebuilding our home on the web.

This site is a redesign of AY Stationery's online presence. The goal is simple: make a large, varied range easy to explore and make ordering effortless, while keeping the personal service our customers know us for.

We kept the brand's existing sky and deep blues and its Poppins and Open Sans type, then gave them a bolder, more playful frame. Thick ink outlines, hand-drawn shadows and stationery shapes make the site feel like a page from a notebook.

  1. An animated welcome

    A five-second, AI-generated brand animation loops silently in the hero, so visitors see what we're about before reading a word.

  2. Shop with prices

    A filterable shop of best sellers with clear prices, a cart and a one-minute checkout that sends the order to our team, with payment on pick-up.

  3. List Builder

    Ready-made school, office and art kits that visitors adjust and send to our team on WhatsApp, a digital version of handing a list across the counter.

  4. AYS Helper chatbot

    An always-available assistant on every page that answers common questions about location, hours, bulk quotes and ordering.

  5. Accessible by design

    Readable contrast, keyboard-friendly controls, reduced motion for visitors who prefer it, and a layout that works on any phone.
